Add ONE of the following near the beginning of your existing root-level `initialize_hf.sh`.

Preferred:

    init-training-master

Equivalent:

    source /a0/init_training_master.sh

Do this after any `set -euo pipefail` line and before OpenOperator/supervisord/application startup.

Do NOT replace the rest of your existing initialize_hf.sh unless you intentionally want to change OpenOperator's startup behavior.

Example:

    #!/usr/bin/env bash
    set -euo pipefail

    init-training-master

    # existing OpenOperator initialization continues below...
